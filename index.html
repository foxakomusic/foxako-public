01 — The story

A discovery in the ruins

Asha, a Nomira from the ExNova-1 reconnaissance squad, enters the abandoned ruins of NOVALIS. Beneath an old bridge, a trail of powered cables leads her into a forgotten tunnel.

Full synopsis · spoilers

“Am I Alive” opens the saga in 2302, long after the Last War. While the rest of her squad moves towards a mysterious source of infinite energy, Asha follows the cables alone.

Hidden inside the tunnel, she discovers a cryogenic chamber containing the last human child. What Asha does not yet fully understand is that the child is also her. The child’s consciousness was duplicated and transferred into Asha. While the original body and mind remained frozen in time, Asha continued to live, learn and grow through an entire lifetime.

The artist

Who is Foxako

Foxako is an electronic music producer and visual artist from French-speaking Switzerland. Drawing from electro house, French Touch, funk and disco, his music blends warm synthesizers, punchy rhythms and fragmented samples, with micro-sampling playing a central role in the way he chops, reshapes and rebuilds sound. Gaming-inspired details, imperfect textures and retrofuturist nostalgia are also part of his identity.

His process often begins with material he composes and records himself, before cutting it into small fragments and transforming it into something entirely new.

Alongside the music, Foxako is building an evolving creative universe that connects his releases through characters, visuals and storytelling. He composes the music, writes the stories and leads the project’s visual direction across graphic design, motion, 3D environments and animation.

A note on my process

The music and visuals of NOVALIS are made without generative AI. I prefer rough edges, imperfections and choices that carry a personal intention. Even when the result is less polished, I want it to feel like something I made, not something generated on my behalf.
